Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about JobReady.

Learn how JobReady works, what Premium includes, how CV exports work, and how to get the most from the platform.

What is JobReady?

JobReady is an AI career platform that helps users build CVs, improve weak sections, tailor applications to job adverts, generate cover letters, prepare for interviews and track job search progress.

Is JobReady free?

JobReady includes free tools with usage limits. Premium unlocks more AI usage, advanced scoring, premium templates and extra career tools.

What does Premium include?

Premium includes higher AI usage, CV fixing, advanced CV scoring, resume tailoring, cover letter tools, premium templates and other advanced application features.

Can I export my CV?

Yes. JobReady supports PDF export. Word export can be added as an additional export option.

Are the CVs ATS-safe?

JobReady templates are designed to be clean, structured and recruiter-friendly. Users should still tailor each CV to the job advert before applying.

Does JobReady guarantee a job?

No. JobReady does not guarantee interviews, offers or employment. It helps users improve applications, identify weaknesses and prepare more effectively.

Can I cancel Premium?

Yes. Users should be able to manage or cancel their subscription through the account or billing page.

Who is JobReady for?

JobReady is for students, graduates, apprentices, career switchers, entry-level applicants and professionals who want a clearer, more structured job application process.

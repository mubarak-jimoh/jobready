About JobReady

We help job seekers build stronger applications, not just better-looking CVs.

JobReady was created to give students, graduates, career switchers and professionals a complete career system: CV building, AI improvement, job matching, recruiter review, interview preparation and application tracking.

Our Mission

Make career progress easier to understand.

Many people struggle because they do not know whether their CV is strong, whether it matches the job, or what recruiters are likely to notice. JobReady gives users clearer feedback, practical improvements and a more structured way to apply.

Who We Help

Built for real job seekers.

JobReady supports students, apprentices, graduates, career changers, entry-level applicants and professionals who want to improve their applications and approach the job market with more confidence.

Why It Exists

Because a CV alone is not enough.

A good application needs a strong CV, targeted keywords, clear evidence, interview preparation and consistent tracking. JobReady brings those pieces together.

JobReady Promise

Clean tools, practical feedback and career support designed to help users apply better.

We do not promise guaranteed jobs. We help users build stronger applications, identify weaknesses and take clearer next steps.

Interview Coach

Answer rules that actually work

Start direct: answer the question in your first sentence.

Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

Show proof: mention outcomes, numbers, feedback or impact.

Stay concise: avoid rambling and finish before the timer ends.

Sound employable: calm, specific, honest and reflective.

Recruiter Warning Signs

What weak answers usually miss

Vague claims without examples.

Too much background before the answer.

No result or lesson learned.

Repeating the CV instead of adding insight.

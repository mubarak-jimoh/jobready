Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information JobReady may collect, how it may be used, and how users can contact us about privacy.

1. Information We Collect

JobReady may collect account information such as name, email address, login details, CV content, uploaded documents, job tracking data, AI prompts, usage activity and payment-related information.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide CV building tools, AI features, account access, premium subscriptions, application tracking, product improvements and customer support.

3. CV and Career Data

Users may enter personal career information, CV content, education, employment history, skills, links and job application details. Users should avoid entering sensitive information that is not needed for job applications.

4. AI Processing

Some features may send user-provided content to AI services to generate suggestions, rewrites, scores or feedback. Users should review all AI-generated content before using it.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers such as Stripe. JobReady does not store full card details directly.

6. Cookies and Analytics

JobReady may use cookies, local storage and analytics tools to keep users logged in, remember preferences, understand usage and improve the platform.

7. Data Security

We aim to protect user data using reasonable technical and organisational measures. No online service can guarantee complete security.

8. Data Retention

We keep user data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and improve the platform.

9. User Rights

Depending on location, users may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of their data. Contact us to request help with privacy rights.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@jobready.com
